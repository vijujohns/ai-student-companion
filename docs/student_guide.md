
# AI Student Companion

This tool helps you study your textbooks.

## Ask AI

Select:

Class → Subject → Chapter

Then type a question.

Example:
Why did Bepin Choudhury forget his memory?

The AI reads the textbook and explains.

## Generate Exam

Enter a topic like:

Chapter 3
Tsunami

The system creates practice questions.

## Update Knowledge

If new textbooks are added:

Click **Rebuild Knowledge Base**.
